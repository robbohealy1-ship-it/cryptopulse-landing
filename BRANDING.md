# 🎨 CRYPTO PULSE SIGNALS - Branding Guide

## Brand Identity

**Name:** CRYPTO PULSE SIGNALS

**Tagline:** Premium Crypto Day-Trading Signal Platform

**Mission:** Deliver elite, high-probability crypto trading signals with institutional-grade analysis

---

## Logo

### Design Elements
- **Circular badge** with gradient border (cyan to orange)
- **Candlestick chart** showing market movement (green/white/red candles)
- **Pulse line** representing market heartbeat and signal detection
- **Typography** - Bold, modern, tech-forward style

### Color Palette

**Primary Colors:**
- Cyan Blue: `#00D4FF` - Technology, trust, precision
- Orange: `#FF8C00` - Energy, action, alerts
- Gradient: Cyan → Orange (represents market dynamics)

**Signal Colors:**
- Green: `#00FF00` - Bullish signals, profits
- Red: `#FF0000` - Bearish signals, stops
- White: `#FFFFFF` - Neutral, clarity

**Background:**
- Dark: `#000000` - Professional, focused
- Chart background: Dark with subtle grid

### Logo Usage

**DO:**
- Use on dark backgrounds for maximum impact
- Maintain aspect ratio
- Ensure clear space around logo
- Use high-resolution versions

**DON'T:**
- Distort or stretch
- Change colors
- Add effects or shadows
- Place on busy backgrounds

---

## Typography

**Primary Font:** Bold, sans-serif (similar to Orbitron or Rajdhani)
- Headers: CRYPTO PULSE (metallic silver/cyan)
- Subtext: SIGNALS (orange/gold accent)

**Body Font:** Clean, readable sans-serif
- Documentation: System fonts
- Dashboard: Streamlit default

**Transparent** - Clear reasoning, honest performance

✅ **Good:**
- "Elite crypto signals with 88%+ confidence"
